Open Pebble AI brings your self-hosted OpenWebUI LLMs to your wrist. Press SELECT, speak your question, and read the answer in chat bubbles. 

NOTE: YOU NEED YOUR OWN SELF-HOSTED LLM OR AN API KEY TO USE THIS APP!. The watch app is a thin client, your phone forwards the dictated text to OpenWebUI, then sends the reply back. 
Configuration lives in the Pebble Core companion app — server URL, API key, model, and more.
